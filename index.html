How Do Documentary Shorts Find Their Audiences?

紀錄短片，如何找到它的觀眾
YuRu Shih 施俞如 Producer & Director of Programming and Marketing, Giloo製片．Giloo 節目與行銷總監
Where I stand我站的位置

I sit on both sides of the problem.

我同時站在問題的兩邊

I program and market films at Giloo, a Taiwanese curation platform for independent, issue-driven cinema.

我在 Giloo 負責節目策劃跟行銷。Giloo 是台灣的策展平台，專門放議題型的獨立電影。

I am also a producer. My latest film, Me and My Condemned Father, opened in Taiwanese cinemas this September.

我同時是製片。我最新的作品《我的爸爸是死刑犯》，今年九月在台灣院線上映。

Where I stand我站的位置

So I have been the person holding a finished film with no idea what comes next, and the person trying to find an audience for someone else's.

我做過片子拍完、不知道下一步要幹嘛的人，也做過要幫別人的片子找觀眾的人
A question from registration來自報名者的提問

“We spent eight months filming. And then what? I want to know about the after.”

「我們花了八個月拍一部片，然後呢？我想知道『然後』。」

I want to spend the next fifteen minutes answering this one question.

接下來十五分鐘，我想用整場來回答這一句話。

The map先看地圖

Where can a finished short actually go?

一部拍完的短片，可以去哪裡？
Festivals

International, regional, issue-based, shorts-only. Different scales, different aims.

影展。國際大型、地區型、議題型，還有短片專門的影展。規模跟訴求都不一樣。

Community screenings

Clubs, classrooms, NGOs, bookshops. Thirty people, four or five shorts in a sitting.

社群放映。社團、教室、NGO、書店。二三十人的場，一次放四五部短片。

Online platforms

License to a streamer or publish it yourself, so the film has somewhere to stay alive.

線上平台。授權給串流平台或自己上架，讓片子有一個長期活著的地方。

Institutional licensing

Schools, libraries, museums, companies. One of the few places a short gets paid.

公播授權。學校、圖書館、美術館、企業。短片少數真的收得到錢的地方。

Broadcast and media

TV channels, online outlets, feature collaborations with news platforms.

電視與媒體。電視台、線上媒體、新聞平台的專題合作。

The next film

A short is your calling card. It can buy feature funding or a commission.

下一部片。短片是你的名片，可以換到長片的資金或一個委製案。

The map先看地圖

More places than most people think. None of them happens on its own.

短片能走的路，比多數人想像中更多。但每一個機會，都需要主動去爭取
Named doors六個可以去洽談的管道

Six doors worth knocking on.

NYT Op-Docs

Pays a licence fee. 30-day exclusive, you keep copyright.

紐約時報。付授權費，獨家 30 天，版權是你的。

The New Yorker Documentary

Finished films under 40 min. Open submissions.

紐約客。40 分鐘內的成片，公開投件。

POV Shorts, PBS

US public TV. 40 min or less, via FilmFreeway, deadline end of July.

美國公視。40 分鐘內，走 FilmFreeway，7 月底截止。

The Guardian Documentaries

Commissions 12 to 20 short docs a year, 15 to 30 min. Pitch via their form.

衛報。一年委製 12 到 20 部，15 到 30 分鐘，官網表單提案。

Short of the Week

Curated platform. Selected films get a feature on the site and social push; some also go on the S/W YouTube channel (2.27M) at the editors’ pick. Must stay online at least 30 days. 40 dollars to submit.

策展平台。入選會在官網刊登專文加社群曝光，部分再由編輯挑上 YouTube 頻道（227 萬訂閱）。片要至少開放線上 30 天。報名 40 美元。

Omeleto

YouTube, near 4 million subscribers, a film a day. Submission fee via FilmFreeway.

YouTube 頻道，近 400 萬訂閱，每天一部。走 FilmFreeway，有報名費。

The problem問題

Three things break at the same time.

三個環節，同時失靈
Audiences

They cannot find the film.

觀眾找不到你的片

They do not know it exists.

他們不知道它存在。

Creators

They cannot sustain the work.

創作者沒辦法靠這件事活下去

Finish one film and you start hunting for the next budget.

拍完一部，還是要重新找下一筆錢。

Market

There is no channel out.

市場沒有出口

More films get made every year. The ways out have not grown.

每年拍出來的片越來越多，管道卻沒有變多。

Why shorts feel it first為什麼短片最先感受到

A short film's life is a few festivals, and then silence.

一部短片的生命，常常就是跑幾個影展，然後就沒了下文

For most shorts there is no theatrical window to approach, no distributor who is interested, and no marketing budget. Submitting to festivals becomes the only goal.

多數短片沒有院線窗口可以聯繫，沒有發行商有興趣，也沒有行銷預算。投稿影展變成唯一的目標。

For a short, the day the festival ends is often the day people stop mentioning it.

對短片來說，影展結束的那一天，常常就是沒有人再提起它的那一天。

The problem

Through my work with filmmakers, festivals, and audiences, I came to see one clear problem.

It is not a content problem. It is a distribution problem.

The films exist. The audiences exist. The path between them is broken.

The broken link斷掉的那一節

Every stage assumes marketing is the next stage's job.

每個環節都以為行銷是下一個環節的事
Development · the grant開發．找補助
→
Shooting · just finish it拍攝．拍完就好
→
Post · the deadline後製．趕影展截止日
→
Festival Run · submit and wait跑影展．投稿、等結果、幾場放映
⇢
Distribution · few buyers發行．很少有發行商會買

The problem isn’t finding an audience. It’s waiting until the film is finished to start. By then, the money is gone, the team has moved on, and the director is exhausted.

問題不是沒有人想找觀眾，而是大家都想等電影做完再找。等真的做完，錢、人和力氣也都用完了。

The broken link斷掉的那一節

So most shorts stop at the festival run. Very few get picked up by a distributor, so you end up promoting the film yourself.

所以大多數短片跑完影展，就停在這裡了。很少有作品會被發行商接手，最後還是得靠創作者自己把作品帶到觀眾面前。

Timing時機

“And then what” cannot wait until the film is finished.

「然後呢」不能等到拍完才想

Whether your shoot runs eight months or three years, you meet people. Your subjects. The organisations around them. The teachers who care about this. The ones who helped you.

不管你拍八個月還是三年，你都會遇到很多人。受訪者、相關的組織、關心這個題目的老師、幫過你的人。

That stretch is the closest you will ever be to these people. After you wrap, you will not run into them again.

那段時間，是你這輩子離這群人最近的時候。拍完之後，你就再也遇不到他們了。

Money, before you shoot開拍之前的錢

The money has the same deadline. Raise it before you shoot.

錢也一樣，開拍前就要解決，不要拍完才想

Crowdfunding, community fundraising, and pre-selling rights can reduce the financial pressure from the start. Don’t borrow to shoot and hope the box office pays it back.

募資、群眾集資、預售版權，可以從一開始就減輕資金壓力。不要先借錢拍片，再期待票房幫你把錢賺回來。

A campaign is not only money. Everyone who backs you already cares about this subject, so you finish the film already holding a list.

募資不只是拿到錢。每個支持你的人，都是已經在乎這個題目的人，你拍完就已經有一份名單。

Why shorts get made為什麼要拍短片

Many shorts are made as a calling card, and the return arrives as funding or a commission for the next film.

很多人拍短片是為了當名片，回報出現在下一部片的資金或委製案上
Why relationships為什麼要經營關係

Festivals bring people together. Theatres bring audiences in. Neither keeps the relationship going.

影展讓人相聚，戲院讓人進場，但兩者都很難讓觀眾持續參與

When a festival ends, everyone goes home. When a viewer leaves the theatre, the relationship usually ends there.

影展一結束，大家就各自回家。觀眾一走出戲院，這段關係往往也就結束了。

You need a place where the relationship can continue.

你需要一個地方，讓觀眾和作品的關係可以繼續。

A question一個問題

Why build your own audience from day one, instead of leaving it to a distributor?

為什麼從第一天就要養自己的觀眾，而不是交給發行商？
Why it has to be you為什麼必須是你自己

Their list never becomes your list.

發行商的名單，不會變成你的名單
You won't know them

You never see who actually responded.

你不會知道你的觀眾是誰

The dashboard, the post metrics, the people replying in the comments. If you never look, you never learn who the film reached.

後台數據、貼文成效、留言裡回話的人。你自己不去看，就永遠不會知道這部片打到誰。

They won't show up

Nobody buys a ticket for a stranger.

他們不會為你走進戲院

With no prior contact and no relationship, nobody buys a ticket just to support you.

沒有互動過，沒有建立過關係，他們不會特地買一張票去支持你。

Next film, from zero

The press contacts and the corporate buyers stay with them.

下一部片，你還是從零開始

Press contacts and corporate buyers stay with the distributor. Your next film starts over.

媒體資源、包場的企業名單，都留在片商手上。下一部片你又要從頭來過。

A real case一個真實的例子

Six years later, same subject, and we started from zero.

六年後，同一個題目，我們從零開始

A director I work with released a film on this subject six years ago, with a large following on Facebook and Instagram.

我合作的一位導演，六年前有一部同樣題目的片，臉書跟 IG 累積了非常多粉絲。

Six years on, different protagonist, same subject, and we opened both accounts from zero. Everything he built stayed with the previous distributor.

六年後，不同的主角，同一個題目，兩個帳號都從零開始。他累積的東西，都留在上一個發行商身上。

Building those relationships is what a distributor is good at, and it is real value. The list just does not copy itself over to the filmmaker.

累積這些關係是發行商的專業，也是他們的價值。問題是這份名單不會自動複製一份給創作者。

A real case一個真實的例子

You have to ask for it. Nobody hands it over on their own.

想把這些觀眾關係留在自己手上，就得主動爭取。沒有人會自動交給你
Six years at Giloo在 Giloo 六年

Three things we learned.

我們學到的三件事
01

Curation beats the algorithm.

策展勝過演算法。一個人指著一部片說「你應該看這個」，比任何推薦系統都有用。

02

People pay for meaning.

人們願意為意義付費。觀眾會為了想懂一件事而花錢。

03

Community out-distributes ads.

社群比廣告更會傳播。一部片透過喜歡它的人往外走，會比任何廣告預算走得遠。

Evidence兩個數字

Two numbers.

95%
of Giloo's visitors arrive on their own. Only five percent come through paid ads.

Giloo 的訪客有九成五是自己找上來的，只有百分之五是廣告帶來的。

1K & 10K
One night with an arts festival. Over a thousand people in the room, over ten thousand watching online at the same time.

跟一個藝術節合作的晚上，現場一千多人，線上同時超過一萬人在看片。

What actually remains真正留下來的

What that night left behind was the list.

那一晚真正留下來的資產，是名單

Online viewers had to register, so we had a way to reach them again. We could keep talking to them after the event.

因為線上觀看需要報名，我們拿得到聯絡方式。事後我們可以繼續跟這些人說話。

A screening of fifty people that collects no contact details makes those fifty people disappear for good.

一場五十個人的放映，如果沒有留下任何聯絡方式，那五十個人就永遠消失了。

Social media, used properly社群媒體真正的用途

Social media is where you find named people, not where you post into the air.

社群媒體不是拿來廣撒訊息，而是幫你找到對的人
Amplifiers

They make more people aware of the film.

能幫你放大聲量的人

Press, critics, creators with reach. They make more people aware the film exists.

媒體、影評、有影響力的創作者。他們讓更多人知道這部片存在。

Activators

They physically bring people into the room.

能真的把人帶到現場的人

Teachers, NGO staff, club officers, book club hosts. One email, one meeting at a time.

老師、NGO 工作者、社團幹部、讀書會召集人。一次聯繫、一場會面，逐步把人聚集起來。

Start now現在就能做

Three things you can start during production.

從拍攝期就能開始做的三件事
01Open a list today.

Every time someone reacts to the subject, write down who they are and why they care.

現在就開一份名單。每遇到一個對這個題目有反應的人，就記下他是誰、為什麼在乎。

02Collect contacts at every screening.

This is the cheapest chance you will ever get.

每一場放映都要留下聯絡方式。這是你最便宜的一次機會。

03Decide which existing conversation your film joins.

It does not need to speak to everyone, only to the people who already care.

想清楚你的片子要放進哪一個已經存在的討論裡。它只需要跟真正在乎的那群人說話。

Money, after you finish拍完之後的錢

Shorts almost never recoup at the box office. Institutions are who pay.

短片幾乎不可能靠票房回本。真正付錢的是機構

Schools, libraries, NGOs, foundations, and corporate social responsibility programmes. That is also how Giloo earns: individual subscriptions and rentals on one side, group licences on the other.

學校、圖書館、NGO、基金會、企業社會責任專案。Giloo 的收入也是這個結構。

What you are really building你真正累積的東西

The asset you are building is the list, and the people who remember you.

你真正累積的資產，是那份名單，跟那群記得你的人

The rest of the return arrives on the next film, as a commission or a backed campaign.

其他的回報出現在下一部片，變成委製案或集資的支持。

The last thing最後一件事

Make the film you want to make. Then choose how it meets people.

Skip the festivals, put it on YouTube, and go for the biggest audience.

你可以不投影展，直接放上 YouTube，衝最大的觀看數和社群聲量。

Skip every streaming platform and aim only at the festivals you care about.

你也可以不上任何串流，目標只放在入圍你在乎的影展。

Both are right. It depends on how you want to share it, what you want from it, and who should see it.

兩條路都對。取決於你想怎麼分享這部片、你要的是什麼、你想讓誰看到。

That is the healthiest way to stay in this industry.

這是在這個產業裡，最健康的活法。
